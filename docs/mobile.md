# Phones

Perps is in the dock at the bottom of the screen: Feed, Launch, Coins, **Perps**, Wallet.

## The layout

From top to bottom:

1. **Market header**: the market name (tap it for the market list), price and 24h change, then 24h volume, open interest and funding.
2. **Venue switch**, when more than one venue lists the coin.
3. **Chart**, a little under half the screen, with the interval strip and the Live | TradingView switch.
4. **Balance**: your perps balance and **Deposit**.
5. **Positions, Orders, History**.
6. **Long** and **Short** buttons fixed above the dock. On spot they read **Buy** and **Sell**.

Tapping **Long** or **Short** opens the order ticket as a sheet from the bottom, already on that side. The deposit sheet opens the same way.

Position rows on a phone show the coin, side and leverage, PnL and **Close**. The market list keeps the market and price; 24h change and volume show on wider screens.

## On a computer

The chart and the ticket sit side by side, with your balance above the ticket. Positions, Orders and History run across the bottom.

## Wallets on a phone

Use your wallet's in-app browser, or a wallet that connects to the browser you use. The site never asks for your recovery phrase.

The Hyperliquid trading key is kept in the browser you enabled it in. A wallet's in-app browser and your phone's own browser are separate, so each asks you to **Enable trading** once.
