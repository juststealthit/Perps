# Portfolio

Portfolio, the **Wallet** page, has a **Perps** card next to your spot holdings.

## What the card shows

| Item | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Account value | Your Hyperliquid account value plus the collateral and PnL of your Jupiter positions |
| Unrealized PnL | Open profit or loss across every position |
| Positions | Up to four open positions: coin, venue, side, leverage, size and PnL |

Tap a position to open the Perps page on that coin. Tap the **Perps** title to open the Perps page.

With nothing open and no balance, the card says "Your perps positions on Hyperliquid and Jupiter show here." with an **Open Perps** button.

## Which accounts it reads

The same as the Perps page: the Hyperliquid account of your connected EVM wallet, and the Jupiter positions of your connected Solana wallet. Connect both to see both.
