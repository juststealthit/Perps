# FAQ

## Basics

**Does Just Stealth It hold my money?**
No. Your positions are on Hyperliquid or Jupiter, under your own wallet's address. Orders are signed in your browser. See [Safety and risk](safety-and-risk.md).

**Which venues can I trade today?**
Hyperliquid, from an EVM wallet, and Jupiter Perps, from a Solana wallet. Aster's markets and charts are live, and Aster trading is coming soon.

**Can I trade stocks or commodities?**
Aster lists some as perps, and they show in the market list. Trading them here comes with Aster trading.

**Is spot trading here?**
Yes, Hyperliquid's spot pairs. Switch the market list to **Spot**.

**What does it cost?**
Each venue charges its own trading fees, plus funding or borrow fees while a position is open. The ticket shows a Fees estimate before you trade. Deposits show the bridge cost in the quote. See [Venues](venues.md#fees).

## Wallets

**I only have Phantom. Can I trade?**
Yes, on Jupiter: SOL, ETH and BTC. For Hyperliquid, connect an EVM wallet too and fund it with SOL from Phantom. See [Solana users on Hyperliquid](wallets.md#solana-users-on-hyperliquid).

**Will I need an EVM wallet forever for Hyperliquid?**
No. Perps accounts for Solana wallets are coming soon.

**What am I signing when I press Enable trading?**
A trading key for this browser, and a one-time approval so orders can be placed from this site. Neither moves funds, and the key can't withdraw. See [Approvals](wallets.md#one-time-approvals-on-hyperliquid).

**Why don't I get a wallet prompt for each Hyperliquid order?**
The trading key you approved signs them. That's how Hyperliquid's own app works too.

**Why is it asking me to Enable trading again?**
The trading key lives in one browser. A new browser, a new device or cleared site data needs a new one.

**Why can't I Enable trading?**
Hyperliquid accepts nothing from an account that's never deposited. Deposit first.

## Orders

**Why does the Jupiter button stay off?**
Jupiter needs at least $10 of collateral. Pay a bit more SOL.

**Why is there no Limit on Jupiter?**
Jupiter trades here are market orders only.

**Why was my order refused?**
The toast shows the venue's own reason. Common ones: not enough balance, a size under the market's minimum step, or the price moved past the slippage limit.

**Is the liquidation price exact?**
On Jupiter it's Jupiter's own quote. On Hyperliquid the ticket shows an estimate, since cross margin depends on your whole account. Positions shows Hyperliquid's real figure once you're in.

**Can I set take profit or stop loss?**
Not yet. Coming soon.

**Can I close part of a position?**
Not from this page yet. **Close** closes all of it. Hyperliquid's and Jupiter's own apps can close part.

## Money

**How do I withdraw?**
Withdrawals from the Perps page are coming soon. Your Hyperliquid account is yours, so you can withdraw on [app.hyperliquid.xyz](https://app.hyperliquid.xyz) with the same wallet. Jupiter pays back in SOL when you close.

**My deposit is taking a while.**
Relay usually credits within the time the quote shows. The sheet follows it until it's credited or refunded. A refund goes back to the wallet that paid.

**Can I deposit USDC directly?**
The sheet takes SOL, or your EVM wallet's native coin. Relay turns it into USDC on Hyperliquid.

## Charts and data

**Why does the Jupiter chart say Hyperliquid?**
Jupiter has no candles of its own, so the chart shows the same coin on Hyperliquid, labelled.

**Why does TradingView show a slightly different price?**
TradingView may not carry the venue, so it shows the same instrument from another source. The header and ticket show the venue's own price.

**A coin has letters instead of a logo.**
No icon was found for it. It's the same market.
